Support & Assistance

Developer: vault. • Self-Custody Multi-Chain Wallet

1. What does non-custodial mean?

Non-custodial means you, and only you, own and control the cryptographic keys to your wallet. VaultETH never has access to your funds, private keys, or recovery phrase.

2. Can you recover my wallet if I lose my 12-word recovery phrase?

No. Your recovery phrase is generated directly on your device and stored in the hardware-backed Secure Enclave and Keystore. It is never sent to any server. If you lose your recovery phrase and delete the app, no one can restore your wallet. Always store your phrase safely offline.

3. Does VaultETH charge transaction fees?

VaultETH charges zero platform fees. 100% of the network fee displayed during transactions is paid directly to blockchain network validators for transaction execution.

4. Why does VaultETH require Biometrics or Device Passcode?

Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode is used by your device's hardware keystore to protect your recovery phrase from unauthorized access. Every key reveal or transfer authorization prompts for verification.
