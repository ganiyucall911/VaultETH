Privacy Policy

Developer: vault. • Last Updated: October 2026

VaultETH is an open, sovereign, self-custodial multi-chain wallet application developed by vault. Your privacy and financial self-sovereignty are the fundamental design principles of VaultETH.

1. Zero Personal Data Collected

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell your personal information, name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers. The application contains zero analytics frameworks, trackers, or advertising SDKs.

2. Hardware-Backed On-Device Security

Your BIP-39 recovery phrases and private cryptographic keys are generated and stored exclusively within the local device's hardware Secure Enclave (iOS Keychain) and Android Keystore (AES-256 GCM). They are never synced to cloud backups, remote servers, or external services.

3. Direct Decentralized RPC Communication

To display balances, gas fee estimates, and broadcast transactions, VaultETH communicates directly over HTTPS with public blockchain JSON-RPC endpoints. RPC providers never receive your private keys or recovery phrase.

4. Public Blockchain Records

Transactions broadcast via VaultETH are recorded permanently and immutably on decentralized public blockchains as required by blockchain protocols.

5. Contact & Assistance

For questions or verification regarding this Privacy Policy, users can access the built-in Support & FAQ section inside the VaultETH application (Settings > Support & FAQ).
