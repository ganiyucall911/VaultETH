Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers to Common Questions

Who controls the keys to my VaultETH wallet? ▼
You do. VaultETH is strictly self-custodial. When you create a wallet, a standard 12-word BIP-39 recovery phrase is generated locally on your device. The private keys are encrypted and stored inside your hardware Secure Enclave or Android Keystore. We never see or store your keys.
How do I add or purchase crypto tokens? ▼
You can purchase tokens directly within VaultETH using Apple Pay, Google Pay, credit/debit cards, or bank transfers via regulated on-ramp partners. You can also transfer crypto from any external exchange or wallet to your derived deposit addresses.
Why don't I need to manually configure gas fees? ▼
VaultETH automatically queries the blockchain via EIP-1559 base fee and priority fee algorithms to ensure your transfer confirms quickly without overpaying. You see a clear, transparent USD estimate before confirming.
Can I import my existing wallet from MetaMask or Phantom? ▼
Yes. VaultETH supports importing any standard 12 or 24-word BIP-39 recovery seed phrase or individual private keys. All your Ethereum, Solana, and multi-chain tokens will appear immediately.
How does ENS (.eth) resolution work in VaultETH? ▼
VaultETH connects directly to Ethereum's official ENS Universal Resolver smart contract over JSON-RPC. When you type any name ending in .eth, it verifies the checksum and resolves both forward and reverse records instantaneously with zero middlemen.
